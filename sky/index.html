SKY 1DTE

Every trading day 15 minutes before the close, sell 10 SPX iron condors expiring the next trading day: shorts at the |delta| nearest 0.20 without going over, longs 5 points out. On expiry day a 5-minute close through a short rolls the other side to delta 0.20, moved closer to price until the new spread takes $0.70, stopping short of the tested short. From 13:00 ET, a close through that side's long moves the other short onto the tested short if that nets $0.30 after the buy-back: an iron fly held to expiry. Settled at intrinsic against SPX's close. Paper, mid fills; P/L is net of commission: $3.50 a condor at the entry and $3.50 a spread for each roll or conversion, per contract.
